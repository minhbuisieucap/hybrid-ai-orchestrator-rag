## Refunds

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of receiving a product.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item has
been inspected. Digital products are refundable only when they have not been
downloaded or activated.

## Warranty

Hardware products include a twelve-month limited warranty from the delivery
date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but does not cover accidental
damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes three to five business days. Express shipping takes one
to two business days. Delivery delays caused by weather or carrier disruptions
do not qualify for a shipping refund.

## Support

Support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 local time.
Customers should include their order number when contacting support.
